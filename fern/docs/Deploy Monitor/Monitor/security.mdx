## Trust Service Criteria

SOC 2 focuses on five key areas:

- **Security** — Protecting systems and data from unauthorized access.
- **Availability** — Ensuring systems are available and reliable.
- **Processing Integrity** — Ensuring data is processed accurately and completely.
- **Confidentiality** — Protecting sensitive and confidential information.
- **Privacy** — Managing personal information appropriately.

## SOC 2 Type I and Type II

**SOC 2 Type I** evaluates whether an organization's controls are appropriately designed at a specific point in time.

**SOC 2 Type II** evaluates both the design of controls and whether those controls operate effectively over a period of time.

## Why SOC 2 Matters

SOC 2 helps organizations build customer trust, strengthen security practices, manage risks, and demonstrate that they have established processes for protecting sensitive information.

Common SOC 2 controls include access management, multi-factor authentication, employee security training, incident response procedures, change management, and regular access reviews.
